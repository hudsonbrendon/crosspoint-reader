Built for reading, not features.

01 — WHY
/01

Featherweight

Engineered for ~380 KB of RAM. Stability and fast refreshes come before everything else.

/02

Typography that breathes

Serif and sans faces, adjustable spacing, paragraph indents, and a clean page that respects the words.

/03

Three ways to update

Flash over USB from this page, drop a file on the SD card, or pull updates over Wi-Fi. Your choice.

/04

Reading, tracked

Streaks, finished books, and a flashcard deck for what you want to remember — opt in, never noisy.

/05

A Pomodoro on paper

A focus timer designed for e-ink: minute-resolution refreshes and a full-screen flash between phases.

/06

Open, all the way down

Community firmware. Read the source, file an issue, send a patch. No accounts, no telemetry.
